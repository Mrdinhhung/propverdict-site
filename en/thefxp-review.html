TheFXP (FXP) Review: Instant Funding, 1-Step, 2-Step & Profit Split (2026)

Updated: Nov 23, 2026 · Data source: thefxp.com

TheFXP (brand "FXP") is a prop trading firm run by ALPHAEDGE PRO – FZCO, registered at Dubai Silicon Oasis (DSO), IFZA Properties, Dubai, UAE. Per its homepage, FXP has operated since 2024 and self-reports over 150,000 active traders and more than $11,000,000 in total rewards processed — this is self-published data PropVerdict has not independently verified.

Important note: TheFXP states on its homepage that it is not a financial institution and does not sell financial products or services. Traders are given demo accounts with simulated funds; all trading activity happens in a simulated environment. Profits generated are paid out as a real-money "reward" once a trader meets the required conditions.

Key highlights

  • No consistency rule on the "Zero" package — see the general concept in What Is Drawdown in Prop Trading? and What Is a Consistency Rule?
  • Profit split up to 100% per the homepage (exact conditions vary by package).
  • 100% refundable fee: the challenge fee is reimbursed after your first reward.
  • Keep 20% of the reward during the challenge: traders get rewarded partially even before completing the evaluation phase — a feature not common among traditional prop firms.
  • One free reset after your first reward if you fail.
  • Overnight/weekend holding, EAs, high-frequency trading (HFT) and news trading are allowed.
  • 0% commissions, 24/7 support via email and Discord.

Rewards / payouts

TheFXP processes rewards through third-party payment system "Rise." The homepage states two different timeframes in different places: "Rewards in 24H" in the top banner and "48H Reward" in the detailed benefits section — PropVerdict notes this inconsistency and recommends traders confirm the actual processing time via the FAQ or support before expecting a specific timeframe.

An objective look

TheFXP self-reports a 4.8/5 rating from roughly 5,000 reviews right on its homepage — this is a self-selected figure, not an independent citation from Trustpilot. When PropVerdict checked independent reviews, some traders reported negative experiences with payout requests being denied for an alleged "trading policy violation," while disputing that any violation occurred. This type of dispute is common across many demo-funded prop firms, not unique to TheFXP, but traders should read the allowed/prohibited trading rules carefully before fully trusting marketing claims like "reward on demand" or "fast payout."

PropVerdict's take

TheFXP suits traders who want a low entry cost (thanks to frequent promotions) and like the "keep 20% of the reward during the challenge" mechanism — a relatively rare perk in the industry. However, the marketing figures (rating, total rewards, processing time) are all self-published and show some inconsistency even on the same page; combined with some independent reports of denied payouts, traders should read the Terms & Conditions and Refund Policy carefully before buying, and should not treat this as real capital — it remains a simulated account rewarded with real money once conditions are met.
