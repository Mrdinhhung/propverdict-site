TradeThePool Review: Fees, Drawdown, Profit Split & Scaling Plan (2026)

Updated: 2026-11-20 · Source: tradethepool.com

TradeThePool is a prop trading firm operated by Five Percent Online Ltd (registered in the UK) — part of the same ownership group as The5ers, a long-running prop firm. TradeThePool offers 5 challenge account sizes ranging from $5,000 to $200,000.

Important note: Like most other prop trading firms, trading at TradeThePool takes place on a simulated (demo) environment — "funded" accounts use virtual capital, not real capital traded directly on the market. Profit generated on the simulated account is paid out to traders in real money once payout conditions are met.

Account sizes, fees & challenge conditions

CriteriaValue
Account sizes$5,000 – $200,000 (5 tiers)

Max Loss (overall loss limit)4%

Two challenge tracks: Flex and Max

  • Flex: no time limit to complete the challenge, suited to traders who prefer to trade slowly and stay disciplined rather than race against a deadline.
  • Max: limited to 60 days, paired with a stricter consistency rule than the Flex track.

Profit split & scaling plan

TradeThePool applies a 70/30 profit split (trader keeps 70%) on funded accounts. The firm runs a scaling program for strong performance: for example, a $200,000 account that hits a 10% profit target gets its buying power increased from $200,000 to $210,000, with the Daily Pause limit adjusted up from $4,000 to $4,400 accordingly.

About the operating entity

TradeThePool is owned by Five Percent Online Ltd (UK) — the same group behind The5ers. This is worth noting as traders weigh the group's track record and reputation before signing up, alongside checking the latest terms on the official website themselves.

PropVerdict's take

TradeThePool suits traders who want the flexibility to choose between trading without a time deadline (Flex) and trading with a clear timeline (Max), combined with a fairly low starting entry fee ($59 at the smallest account size). The 2% Daily Pause is relatively tight compared to some other firms, so traders need to manage daily risk carefully. The scaling program is a plus for traders looking to grow their account size over time. As with any prop firm, traders should always double-check the latest terms on the official website before signing up and should not expect guaranteed profit.
