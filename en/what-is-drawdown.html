What Is Drawdown in Prop Trading? Daily vs Max Drawdown

Updated: 2026-11-20 · PropVerdict

Drawdown is the drop in account equity measured against a reference point, used by prop firms as a mandatory risk-management rule. It is the single most common reason traders fail a challenge — more common than simply missing the profit target — so understanding it properly before signing up with any firm is close to essential.

What is Daily Drawdown?

Daily Drawdown caps the maximum loss allowed within one trading day. The reference point used to calculate it can be:

  • Start-of-day balance: the most common and easiest method to track.
  • Highest intraday equity: some firms calculate against the highest equity point reached during the day (including open floating profit), which makes the real limit tighter while a large winning trade is still open.

The calculation method varies significantly between firms and can meaningfully change the real-world risk of the same percentage figure — this is one of the terms worth reading most carefully before buying a challenge.

What is Max Drawdown?

Max Drawdown (also called Total or Overall Drawdown) caps the maximum loss measured from the starting balance (or the highest equity ever reached), applied across the entire challenge or funded account lifetime, not limited to a single day. Some firms use a "trailing drawdown" model — the reference point moves up with the highest equity ever reached, which narrows the real risk allowance as the account becomes profitable.

Why drawdown breaches are the top reason for failing

  • Traders often manage risk based on personal habit (e.g. accepting a 5%/day loss) without checking it against the specific rule of the firm they signed up with.
  • Calculating against the "highest intraday equity" can cause an unexpected breach when a large winning trade reverses, even if the day still closes positive.
  • Trailing drawdown narrows the real risk allowance over time as the account grows, requiring continuous position-sizing adjustments.

How to check drawdown rules before signing up

Before buying a challenge at any firm, verify: whether drawdown is calculated against balance or highest equity, whether trailing drawdown applies, the exact daily and max drawdown percentages, and whether the rules differ between the challenge phase and the funded phase.
