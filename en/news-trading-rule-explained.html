What Is a News Trading Rule? Prop Firm News Restrictions Explained

Updated: Nov 26, 2026 · PropVerdict

A news trading rule is a condition most prop trading firms set to control how traders open or hold positions around the release of major economic news — for example Non-Farm Payroll (NFP), CPI, or FOMC rate decisions. The main reason: around these releases, prices can slip sharply and move erratically, making it harder for a firm to manage systemic risk and to detect traders exploiting pricing glitches without a clear rule in place.

Common types of news rules

  • A full ban within a fixed window: for example, all positions must be flat from 1–5 minutes before to 1–5 minutes after a release, applying to a specific list of "high-impact" news (usually NFP, CPI, FOMC, major energy/agricultural reports).
  • Allowed during Evaluation, restricted once Funded: many firms relax the rule while a trader is still in the evaluation phase (lower risk to the firm since there's no real/simulated capital at stake yet), then tighten it once the account is funded.
  • No named-news list, just a general window: some firms simply state "no new positions within X minutes around any high-impact news for the pair you're trading," without listing specific events.

Real examples from firms PropVerdict has checked

Since each prop firm sets its own rule, the figures below come from PropVerdict's own verified reviews of each firm's official site — this is not one universal rule applying to every firm:

  • Funded Futures Network (FFN): allows news trading during Evaluation, but bans trading around "T1" news (FOMC, FOMC Minutes, NFP, CPI, EIA energy reports, agricultural reports) — positions must be flat from 1 minute before to 1 minute after the release.
  • ICFunded: allows news trading during Step 1 and Step 2, but restricts it to a ±3 minute window around releases once the account reaches the Funded stage.
  • AxionFunded: states "News trading enabled" on a sample widget on its homepage, but the detailed per-model/per-stage Challenge Rules matrix did not render a clear value for this item when PropVerdict checked — a good reminder that traders shouldn't rely on an illustrative widget and should instead read the exact rules table for the package they bought.

Why breaking a news rule is serious

Unlike many other violations (for example, hitting the daily loss limit, which usually just pauses trading for the day), breaking a news trading rule at many firms is classified as a "major violation" that can get a funded account closed immediately — even if the trade that triggered it was profitable. This is something newer traders often overlook, assuming that managing risk by account percentage alone is enough.

How to avoid breaking a news trading rule

  • Always check an economic calendar before entering a trade, and cross-reference it against the specific list of events your prop firm bans — this list differs between firms.
  • Close or exit positions manually, or use an auto-close feature, at least a few minutes before the restricted window starts, to account for processing delays and slippage.
  • Check which stage the rule applies to (Evaluation or Funded) — many firms relax the rule during evaluation but tighten it once funded, as with FFN and ICFunded above.
  • Don't rely on homepage marketing copy or illustrative widgets — always read the official "Rules" or "Trading Objectives" table for the specific account package you purchased.
