Funded Trading Plus (FT+) Review: Instant, 1-Step, 2-Step & Payout (2026)

Updated: Nov 28, 2026 · Data source: fundedtradingplus.com

Funded Trading Plus (FT+) is a brand of Acello Ltd, a company registered in England & Wales (company no. 12696083, registered office 30 Old Bailey, London, EC4M 7AU). Per the footer legal disclosure, Acello Ltd acts as payment agent for IF Pro Ltd, a company registered in Saint Lucia (reg. no. 2025-00056) — the entity that actually provides the "simulated trading programs," with no brokerage service or real trading accounts involved. On brand history, the About page states: the predecessor Trade Room Plus (a trading-education room, once the UK's largest such room on YouTube) was founded in 2013; the Funded Trading Plus brand officially launched in 2021, introducing the "No Time Limit Challenge" model. Leadership is listed as CEO Simon M. (Simon Massey), COO James F., and CMO Michael C.

Important note: FT+ states: "All trading accounts are simulated evaluation accounts. No real capital is traded and no actual market orders are executed... Funded Trading Plus is not authorised or regulated to provide financial services by any regulatory body in any jurisdiction." All "payouts"/"earnings" mentioned on the site are performance-based rewards paid from FT+'s own funds, not withdrawals of real trading capital.

Programs & pricing example ($100,000 1-Step Express)

FT+ offers three main models: Instant Funding, 1-Step Express and 2-Step Classic, plus a newer product labeled "NEW": Activation Accounts, a "Pass Now, Pay Later" model — choose a $25K/$50K/$100K account, pay $4 to start a one-phase challenge (2% target), and only pay the remaining fee after passing. One thing that doesn't match: a marketing banner on the site reads "Up to 100% Reward Split," but the add-on table only lets you buy up to a 90% maximum — PropVerdict found no path in the checked content to an actual 100% split.

Payouts & self-reported figures

The homepage reports "$19.5M+ Total Trader Rewards" since 2021–2026, "180 Countries," "211K+ Accounts Issued," and "60K+ Active Traders." On record payouts specifically, there's a discrepancy: an animated stat tile on the homepage reads "Highest Single Trader Reward: $189,083," while the About page (under "Today") states "the largest single payout to date is $183,000" — these two self-reported figures don't match. The About page also lists milestones: surpassing 25,000 traders in 2022, crossing $10M in total payouts in 2023, and growing to 50+ staff across 14 countries in 2024.

Self-reported awards & ratings

FT+ displays four award badges: "Most Trusted Prop Firm – Prop Firm Stats 2025," "Best Instant Funding – Funded Trading 2025," "Most Trusted UK Firm – Int. Business Mag 2025," and "Best Prop Firm – World Business 2025," alongside claims of "consistent 5-star TrustPilot ratings" and a "98%+ payout approval rate." PropVerdict has not independently verified these awards or figures — the checked content contained no links to third-party confirmation for any specific award. The site also features an "FTP vs. Standard Industry" comparison table that FT+ authored itself — a promotional framing, not a neutral industry benchmark.

Platforms, tools & trading limits

  • Platforms: Match Trade, MT5.
  • News trading: "Allowed – subject to policy" on the 1-Step Express example; the marketing comparison section states broadly "Trade news, hold weekends, and hedge freely."
  • Swap-free: swap-free accounts are offered (stated directly on the example pricing table).
  • Simulated capital scaling: advertised as "Scale simulated balances up to $2.5 million in virtual capital."

On legal compliance, the site lists a fairly extensive sanctions/AML exclusion list (Belarus, Crimea, Russia, Afghanistan, North Korea, Myanmar, Cuba, Syria, Iran, and many more) applied by Acello Ltd — a transparency point worth noting, as the list is more detailed than many other prop firms PropVerdict has reviewed.

Trader reviews (displayed on FT+'s own site)

FT+ displays numerous self-collected trader reviews on its own page (not pulled directly from Trustpilot within the content checked). The most recurring positive theme is satisfaction with payout speed and customer support, with no penalty for trading around news; the most recurring complaint is the trailing drawdown mechanic ("the max drawdown increase as you make profit until you reach account balance" — per a trader named Hamza). This is a quote from a self-displayed review on FT+'s own site; PropVerdict has not independently verified the authenticity of individual reviewer accounts.

An objective look

FT+ has a fairly clear brand history (predecessor from 2013, FT+ brand since 2021) and a transparently disclosed legal structure (Acello Ltd as payment agent for IF Pro Ltd). That said, PropVerdict found two inconsistencies across FT+'s own pages: (1) the highest-ever payout figure differs between the homepage ($189,083) and the About page ($183,000); (2) the "Up to 100% Reward Split" banner doesn't match the actual 90% maximum shown in the add-on table. The trust badges/awards displayed are all self-reported, with no third-party verification links found in the content PropVerdict checked. On the positive side, the swap-free policy, free news trading, and the fairly thorough AML/sanctions exclusion list are good transparency signals.

PropVerdict's take

Funded Trading Plus is a prop firm with a relatively long operating history (since 2021, with educational roots from 2013), offering a diverse product range (Instant/1-Step/2-Step/"pay later" Activation Accounts) along with fairly permissive trading policies (news trading, swap-free). Before buying, traders should: (1) directly confirm the exact reward split and trailing-drawdown details at checkout, given the mismatch between the marketing banner and the actual add-on table; (2) not take the self-reported record figures or awards at face value without independent verification.
