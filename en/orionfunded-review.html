OrionFunded Review: 5 Programs (Zero, Nova, Standard, Swing, Select), Drawdown & Profit Split (2026)

Updated: Nov 28, 2026 · Data source: orionfunded.com

OrionFunded is a prop trading brand provided, per its footer disclaimer, by two entities: OGM International Ltd., registered at Unit 1, La Place Creole, Rodney Bay Village, Gros Islet, Saint Lucia, and Orion Global FZCO. The exact wording: "OGM International Ltd. ... and Orion Global FZCO provide simulated trading services and educational tools for traders only ... Neither entity acts as a broker or accepts deposits." The homepage doesn't state a specific founding year, only "3 Years in the industry" (implying roughly 2023), alongside self-reported figures of "200,000+ traders" and "$7,347,878 rewards distributed" — PropVerdict has not independently verified these numbers. OrionFunded is also listed on Propfirmmatch.com, a third-party prop-firm directory/comparison site, not an independent auditor.

Important note: Per the official FAQ: "Please note that all accounts we provide to our clients are demo accounts with fictitious funds and any trading is in a simulated environment only." All balances, trades and profit shown on an OrionFunded account are simulated; no real capital or brokerage service is involved.

Minimum trading days: Orion Standard, Swing and Select each require at least 4 trading days during evaluation; Orion Zero and Nova have none. No program requires a minimum number of trading days once funded. Orion Nova specifically runs on a "Pay After You Pass" basis: traders pay a small registration fee (EUR/USD 7) to start the evaluation, the funded-account activation fee is only due after passing (with 30 days to pay), and there's no time limit to complete the evaluation. All five programs allow holding positions overnight and over weekends, in both evaluation and funded phases.

On Orion Zero specifically: trades opened or closed within 5 minutes of a high-impact news event are rewarded at 50% instead of 80%; all other trades in the cycle are unaffected. Notably, the "80% reward split" advertised on the homepage for Orion Standard matches the FAQ rules table — PropVerdict did not find the marketing-vs-rules gap seen at some other prop firms.

Payouts

Per the FAQ, the reward request cycle runs on a 14-day basis from the Orion Trader Account. The homepage advertises: "Get paid in less than 48 hours. Paid late? You get 100% of the reward split and a full refund." — i.e. OrionFunded commits to processing rewards within 48 hours; if paid late, the trader gets a 100% reward split for that cycle plus a full refund of the program fee.

On payment methods, the FAQ "How are rewards paid out?" states rewards are paid as real money via methods available in the Client Section at the time of request, "exact methods and processing times are confirmed in the Client Section before you submit" — meaning OrionFunded does not publish one fixed, public list of payment methods; they're confirmed individually, at the time of request, in the Client Section.

One notable policy: OrionFunded refunds the entire program fee on a trader's 4th successful reward withdrawal from a funded account — this applies to all programs.

  • Swap-free: the FAQ states swap-free accounts are "not available as a standard option" — OrionFunded doesn't offer swap-free accounts as a default choice.
  • News trading: during evaluation there are no timing restrictions around news, but using news trading as a strategy is prohibited across all programs and phases. Once funded: Nova/Standard/Select have a 2-minute blackout window before and after any high-impact event; Swing has no restrictions; Zero allows trading at all times but trades within 5 minutes of a news event are rewarded at 50% instead of 80%.
  • Best Day Rule: applies only to Orion Zero and funded Orion Nova accounts — a trader's single best profit day cannot exceed 20% of total simulated profit within the reward cycle; if it does, the trader must keep trading until that ratio drops to 20% or below before requesting a reward.

An objective look

OrionFunded publishes large self-reported figures ("200,000+ traders", "$7,347,878 rewards distributed") that PropVerdict has not independently verified; the only third-party reference found was a directory listing (Propfirmmatch.com), not an independent auditor. The five-program structure (Zero/Nova/Standard/Swing/Select) is fairly complex, with different drawdown, profit-target and news-trading rules per program — the homepage only shows sample pricing/rules for Orion Standard, so traders need to check the correct rules page for the specific program they buy. The "paid late → 100% reward + full refund" guarantee is a strong, clearly worded commitment, but the exact payment methods aren't published as a fixed list — they're only confirmed in the Client Section at request time, making them hard to verify before purchase. On the positive side, the 80% profit split advertised on the homepage matches the FAQ rules table, with no marketing-vs-rules gap of the kind PropVerdict has flagged at some other firms. The pricing PropVerdict could extract was only available as a buy-2-get-2-free bundle (4 accounts); no standalone single-account price was found in the content checked.

PropVerdict's take

OrionFunded offers a diverse five-program ecosystem suited to different trading styles — from Zero's no-evaluation instant model to Select's three-step path — with clearly published daily-loss/max-drawdown rules per program, and a late-payout guarantee (100% reward plus full refund) that's fairly rare in the industry. That said, traders should weigh a few caveats before buying: the scale figures (trader count, rewards distributed) lack independent verification, specific payment methods aren't published as a fixed list, and the multi-program structure requires reading the exact rules table for the product purchased rather than relying on the homepage's sample display.
