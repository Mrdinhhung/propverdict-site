Funded Futures Network (FFN) Review: MAX, STEADY, Drawdown & Profit Split (2026)

Updated: Nov 24, 2026 · Data source: fundedfuturesnetwork.com

Funded Futures Network (FFN) is a futures prop trading firm operated as Funded Futures Network LLC, with an address listed at 99 Wall Street #1660, New York, NY, USA. Per its homepage, FFN has been "in business since 2022" and self-reports over 300,000 accounts and millions of dollars paid out to traders — this is self-published data PropVerdict has not independently verified. The homepage does not state any financial regulatory registration (e.g. CFTC/NFA in the US).

Important note: Like most futures/forex "demo-funded" prop firms, FFN gives traders simulated accounts during the Evaluation/Exhibition/Sim Funded stages; real money only appears as a "reward/payout" once conditions are met. Only the "FFN Institution" (Live) tier uses the company's own real capital ($3,000, principal not withdrawable).

After passing, the funded (Sim Funded) 50K account keeps the $2,000 EOD drawdown but adds a "Drawdown Lock" (locks at starting balance + $100), a $1,250 daily loss limit, and the same 40% consistency rule. The funded account reset fee (before the first payout) is $625. Minimum balance thresholds required to withdraw (must be $500 above starting capital): $26,500 for 25K; $52,000 for 50K; $103,600 for 100K; $155,000 for 150K; $256,000 for 250K.

Profit split & contract scaling

  • Sim Funded Pro: 80/20 profit split (trader keeps 80%).
  • FFN Institution (Live): 90/10 profit split; traders start with $3,000 of real FFN capital (the principal can never be withdrawn) and get same-business-day payouts with no consistency rule at this stage.
  • Max contract size scales with cumulative profit: 2 Mini (20 Micro) at $0–$1,000 profit; 3 Mini (30 Micro) at $1,001–$2,000; 4 Mini (40 Micro) from $2,001 up.
  • After 4 payouts on one account (or 10 since the last review), FFN performs a "Live Review" — all sim accounts close after this step.

Payouts

At the Sim Funded stage, FFN states "same-day" payouts, provided the account is above the minimum balance threshold and the consistency rule is met. Minimum withdrawal is $500; the per-payout cap on a 50K account is $1,000 (payouts 1–3) and $1,250 (payout 4+); there's also a $10,000-per-payout cap across all of a trader's accounts. Payout methods are PayPal, Rise, or Wise. The site's "payouts" page shows a self-published ledger: 2,846 total payouts, $5,332,455 total paid (updated Nov 23, 2026) — company-reported figures, not independently audited.

Platforms & tradable products

FFN doesn't use MT4/MT5 like forex prop firms; instead it integrates futures-specific platforms: Onyx, Nexus Trader (Nexus Connect), Rithmic, DeepCharts (dxFeed), Quantower, MotiveWave, EdgeProX. Tradable products focus on micro futures: MES (Micro S&P), MNQ (Micro Nasdaq), M2K (Micro Russell), MYM (Micro Dow), MCL (Micro Crude Oil), MBT/MET (Micro Bitcoin/Ether). The full product list and country-specific rules are published in FFN's separate "Rules" page, which PropVerdict has not been able to fully extract — read it carefully before buying.

Rules & restrictions to note

  • News trading: allowed during Evaluation, but T1 news events are banned (FOMC, FOMC Minutes, NFP, CPI, EIA energy reports, agricultural reports) — all positions must be flat from 1 minute before to 1 minute after the release, per the "Financial Juice" calendar.
  • Trading hours: all positions must be closed by 4:50pm EST; the overnight session can reopen at 6:00pm EST.
  • EAs/bots: automated trading is allowed at FFN's discretion; HFT bots or exploiting the simulated environment will result in account closure.
  • Hedging: banned between products in the same family (e.g. ES/NQ) and between mini/micro contracts of the same product.
  • Maximum 5 active Sim Funded accounts at a time; closing an account forfeits any unwithdrawn profit.

An objective look

PropVerdict found no Trustpilot score or independent rating on the FFN pages checked — the homepage only displays the word "Excellent" with no number or citable source. The large figures (300,000+ accounts, millions paid, the payout ledger) are all self-published. FFN's multi-stage structure (Evaluation → Exhibition → Sim Funded → Institution) is more layered than the common 1-step/2-step model seen at forex prop firms, so traders should read carefully to understand what each stage actually entitles them to before buying.

PropVerdict's take

Funded Futures Network suits futures traders who want a path that ends in a "Live Institution" stage backed by the company's own real capital (90/10 split) once they've proven themselves through the sim stages — a differentiator from firms that keep paying simulated "rewards" indefinitely. That said, the advertised pricing is a limited-time promotion, the 40% consistency rule on the MAX Standard tier is fairly strict, and some figures (like the minimum-days comparison table) aren't consistent across the site — traders should check the exact rule table for the package they're buying and treat marketing figures as unverified claims rather than confirmed facts.
