ICFunded Review: 1-Step, 2-Step, Instant Funded & 14-Day Payouts (2026)

Updated: Nov 26, 2026 · Data source: icfunded.com

ICFunded is operated by two affiliated entities: IC Funded Evaluations Ltd, registered in Saint Lucia (registration number 2024-00388, address Unit 1, La Place Creole, Rodney Bay Village, Gros Islet, Saint Lucia), and IC Funded Tech Solutions Ltd, registered in Cyprus (number HE 464324, address Gladstonos 116, M. Kyprianou House, 3rd & 4th Floor, 3032 Limassol, Cyprus) — the site states the Cyprus entity acts as "distributor" of the service, and the two are "affiliated companies under common ownership and are separate legal entities." The homepage does not state an operating-since year or any financial regulatory licence.

Important note: ICFunded states: "IC Funded provides services exclusively in a simulated trading environment using virtual funds. Any accounts provided to users are demo accounts only and do not involve real-market execution, real capital, or the custody of client funds." The site also states it is not a broker, does not accept deposits, and does not provide investment management services.

Account models & sizes

ICFunded offers three models: 1-Step Accelerated (one evaluation phase), 2-Step Professional (two evaluation phases), and Instant Funded (no evaluation). Account sizes: $5,000, $10,000, $25,000, $50,000, $100,000, $200,000, $500,000. The site caps total allocation per trader: "Multiple funded accounts are permitted, with a maximum total allocation of $500,000 per trader."

Pricing (checked Nov 26, 2026)

PropVerdict confirmed a fee of $74 for the 2-Step Professional $5,000 package (one-time fee: "One-time fee: $74 USD"). Fees for other sizes and for the 1-Step/Instant models were not shown as a static table in this check — check ICFunded's own pricing page directly for the exact figure for the size you want. PropVerdict did not see any promotion advertised at the time of checking.

The table above reflects the $5,000 2-Step package per the Rules page; larger sizes may use the same percentages, but PropVerdict has not extracted figures size-by-size. Daily drawdown is calculated on equity, including open positions, not just closed balance — the FAQ says: "calculated based on equity, not only the closed balance." Reset time: "00:00 server time, which corresponds to 00:00 GMT+2 or GMT+3" depending on daylight saving. No mandatory stop-loss is required ("Mandatory stop-loss: Not required"). Leverage: FX up to 1:50; Indices/Commodities/Metals/Energies up to 1:20; Crypto up to 1:2.

News trading & weekend holding

News trading is "Allowed" during Step 1 and Step 2; at the Funded stage it is "Restricted ±3 min" around news releases. Weekend holding is "No" at every stage (Step 1, Step 2 and Funded).

Profit split & fee refund

The standard profit split is 80%. For 1-Step and 2-Step, traders "can increase their profit share up to 90% with selected add-ons" ("Add-on eligibility and conditions apply"). Instant Funded caps out at 80%. Notably, the challenge fee is refunded after the 3rd payout — "For 1-Step and 2-Step, the most recent challenge fee is refunded with the third payout."

Payouts

The standard payout cycle is 14 days ("request eligible profit payouts every 14 days"; the Rules page also calls this a "Bi-weekly payout path · Weekly available as add-on," meaning weekly withdrawals are available as a paid upgrade). Specific conditions per payout:

  • 1st payout: requires 5 profitable days and a minimum of 14 calendar days since the funded account opened.
  • 2nd payout: requires 3 profitable days and a minimum of 14 calendar days.
  • 3rd payout onward: only the 14-calendar-day minimum applies, no profitable-day requirement.

A "profitable day" is defined as a day that closes with at least 0.5% profit on the account's initial balance. PropVerdict was unable to extract the minimum withdrawal amount, specific payout methods, or per-payout caps.

Trading platforms

Per the FAQ, ICFunded offers MetaTrader 5 (MT5) and cTrader, both at no additional cost, accessible via desktop, web and mobile.

An objective look

ICFunded's homepage features a "Verified Trader Payouts" section with sample payout certificates (e.g. "$612.80," "$3,064.80" dated August–September 2026, with names/country flags) — this content is self-displayed on ICFunded's own site with no named independent verifier, so PropVerdict treats it as self-published data. The site also shows two "IC Funded Award" images in the footer with no caption naming the issuing body. PropVerdict found no Trustpilot score on the pages checked. One minor inconsistency to flag: the "How It Works" page lists the 1-Step max drawdown as 6%, while the Rules page (for 2-Step) frames drawdown as "fixed on initial balance" — traders should confirm the exact calculation basis for the 1-Step package before buying.

PropVerdict's take

ICFunded's advantage is naming both operating entities clearly, with specific registration numbers in Saint Lucia and Cyprus — more transparent than many prop firms that only display a brand name. The tiered payout structure (5 profitable days → 3 → no requirement) and the challenge-fee refund after the 3rd payout are good features for newer traders. However, a full per-size price table and specific payout methods aren't clearly published in the static content, so traders should check directly at checkout before deciding.
